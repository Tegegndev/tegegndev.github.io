Sirba: Android Music & Audio Player

A dedicated mobile music player and audio streaming application engineered for Ethiopian and Oromo music (Sirba), equipped with background playback services, dynamic lock-screen controls, and low-bandwidth offline caching.

1. The Problem: Data Constraints & Local Audio Experiences

In Ethiopia, mobile data connectivity can fluctuate in bandwidth, making standard high-bitrate international music streaming platforms data-prohibitive and prone to frequent buffering pauses. Furthermore, listeners seeking Ethiopian cultural songs, regional releases, and Afaan Oromoo music (Sirba) often struggle with fragmented downloads and lack a centralized, lightweight audio player that operates reliably offline.

The Sirba Android App was built to solve these connectivity constraints by providing a streamlined, culturally focused mobile listening experience optimized for data conservation, smart local caching, and seamless background playback.

2. Technical Architecture & Audio Pipeline

Building a robust mobile audio application on Android requires navigating strict operating system battery-saving constraints and background process limits:

Core Audio Pipeline:

Audio Stream Request → Local Cache Verification → Foreground Audio Service → Notification & Lock-Screen Handler → ExoPlayer Audio Sink

  • Foreground Service Execution: Utilizes an Android foreground service with persistent notifications, preventing the Android OS from terminating playback when the user switches apps or locks their screen.
  • Adaptive Offline Caching: Automatically caches requested audio segments to local storage, allowing repeated playback without consuming additional cellular data.
  • MediaSession Integration: Connects directly with Android's system-level MediaSession API, providing seamless compatibility with Bluetooth earphones, car audio heads, and lock-screen seek bars.
  • Lightweight UI Architecture: Clean, responsive user interface with dynamic album art rendering, playlist queue organization, and dark mode optimization to preserve mobile battery.

3. Key Engineering Highlights

A. Battery-Efficient Background Playback

Modern Android versions aggressively kill background tasks to conserve power. Sirba integrates proper audio focus management (pausing smoothly during phone calls and resuming afterwards) and adheres to Android 14+ foreground service types for audio playback.

B. Low-Bandwidth Streaming Resilience

Implements audio buffer preloading and chunked streaming, ensuring playback begins within hundreds of milliseconds even under 3G network conditions.
